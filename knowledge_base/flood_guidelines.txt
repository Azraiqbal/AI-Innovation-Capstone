Flood Emergency Guidelines

1. Avoid walking or driving through floodwater.
2. Disconnect electrical supply only when it is safe to do so.
3. Stay away from fallen electrical wires and submerged electrical equipment.
4. Move affected people to higher and safer ground.
5. Prioritize children, elderly people, injured individuals, and persons with disabilities during evacuation.
6. Contact trained emergency responders when people are trapped.
7. Do not enter floodwater where electrical hazards may be present.
8. Use rescue boats or trained rescue personnel for areas with deep or moving water.
9. Provide first aid and medical assessment after evacuation where required.
10. Monitor water levels and changing weather conditions.
