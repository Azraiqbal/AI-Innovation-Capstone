Earthquake Emergency Guidelines

1. During shaking, drop, cover, and hold on.
2. Stay away from windows, glass, and unstable furniture.
3. After shaking stops, evacuate damaged buildings carefully.
4. Expect aftershocks.
5. Check for injured or trapped people without entering unsafe structures.
6. Avoid damaged electrical lines and gas leaks.
7. Keep emergency routes clear.
8. Do not enter visibly unstable buildings.
9. Prioritize injured and vulnerable individuals during evacuation.
10. Follow instructions from local emergency authorities.
