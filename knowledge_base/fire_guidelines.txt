Fire Emergency Guidelines

1. Raise the alarm and alert emergency services immediately.
2. Evacuate people using the nearest safe exit.
3. Do not use elevators during a building fire.
4. Keep people away from smoke-filled areas.
5. Crawl low under smoke where necessary during evacuation.
6. Do not re-enter the building until authorities declare it safe.
7. Prioritize trapped, injured, elderly, and vulnerable people.
8. Isolate electricity or gas only when it can be done safely.
9. Provide first aid to injured individuals after evacuation.
10. Keep access routes clear for emergency responders.
